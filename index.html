The Story

One house, two voices.

Latrice Latrice started with a simple refusal: that heritage wear has to look like a souvenir, and that quiet luxury has to look like nobody.

Heritage is the loud half — illustrated maps, adinkra, the drum, the raised fist, cotton and indigo. Worn by people who want the conversation.

Essentials is the restraint — heavyweight fleece, cargo detailing, a monogram small enough that you have to be close to read it.

Same name on the label. Different days of the week.
